// MESH NETWORK

How Mesh Works

Devices connect directly to form a decentralized mesh network — no infrastructure required.

01

Discover

Your device scans for nearby peers using Bluetooth Classic and WiFi Direct. The watchdog ensures discovery never stops.

02

Connect

ECDH key exchange establishes an end-to-end encrypted channel. Devices handshake and negotiate the best transport.

03

Communicate

Messages queue locally and transmit through the best route. If a direct path is blocked, messages hop through the mesh.

04

Relay

Every device can act as a gateway. The dedup system prevents duplicate processing across transports.
